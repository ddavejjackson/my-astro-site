## Schizoaffective Disorder
### What is it?
Schizoaffective disorder is a "psychotic illness." It is basically a combination of Schizophrenia and Bipolar disorder or Schizophrenia and Depression.

I have the Bipolar type and it is quite severe. I hope one day to be medication free, but I doubt this will happen as my illness is too severe.
